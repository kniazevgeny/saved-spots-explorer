Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Eugene Kniazev and Mikhail Aizinov (operating as Whimbean, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects personal information when you use our discovery map and related services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1. Account Information: We currently do not require account registration to use the service. If you choose to sign in, we collect your email address and authentication identifiers provided by Google or Apple.

1.2. Geolocation Data: With your explicit permission, we process your precise location to show your position on the Whimbean Map and find nearby spots. We process this data in real-time and do not intentionally maintain a long-term history of your precise movements.

1.3. Usage and Analytics: We use PostHog to understand how you interact with the map (clicks, searches, session duration). This helps us improve the "Vibe Matching" algorithm.

1.4. Cookies: We may use cookies or similar technologies for analytics and basic service functionality.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide the core Discovery Map services.
  • To save your favorite "beans" (locations) to your profile.
  • To improve search relevance and location recommendations.
  • To respond to your feedback and support requests.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We only share limited information with trusted service providers (PostHog for analytics, Cloud hosting providers, and Map providers like Mapbox/Google) to keep the service running. Some providers may process data outside your country of residence using appropriate safeguards.

4. Legal Basis (GDPR & UK GDPR)

We process your data based on your Consent (especially for location access) and our Legitimate Interests in providing and improving an AI-powered discovery tool.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, export, or delete your data at any time. You can also revoke location permissions through your browser or device settings.

6. Data Retention

We retain account and saved location data until you delete your account. Analytics data is retained for a limited period necessary to improve the service.

7. Children's Privacy

The service is not intended for children under 13 (or applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction).

8. Changes to Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the service after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
